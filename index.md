### Unit 2 — Counseling Theories: The Major Schools  ☑ readings + items
_Readings:_
- [unit02-theories](wiki/unit02-theories.md) — hub: the six families, the "stuck because / better by"
  comparison table, time-focus + therapist-stance axes, the integrative landing spot.
- [theory-psychodynamic](wiki/theory-psychodynamic.md) — Freud's lineage; unconscious, defense
  mechanisms, transference; insight as the change mechanism.
- [theory-person-centered](wiki/theory-person-centered.md) — Rogers/Maslow; actualizing tendency,
  conditions of worth, client as expert; the theory form of Unit 1's core conditions.
- [theory-existential](wiki/theory-existential.md) — Yalom's four givens, Frankl's logotherapy,
  May; meaning, freedom, responsibility.
- [theory-cbt](wiki/theory-cbt.md) — Beck & Ellis; cognitive triangle, ABC model, distortions, the
  learning tradition; Ellis-vs-Beck.
- [theory-family-systems](wiki/theory-family-systems.md) — Bowen & Minuchin; identified patient,
  differentiation, triangulation, structure/boundaries; the problem is in the system.
- [theory-postmodern](wiki/theory-postmodern.md) — solution-focused (de Shazer/Berg) & narrative
  (White/Epston); social constructionism, re-authoring, the non-expert stance.

_Items:_ [unit02-theories](items/unit02-theories.md) — 36 items, all in the `theory-families`
interleaving cluster (topic tags per school), all 5 Bloom levels; heavy on **compare-and-contrast**,
**differential vignettes (mcq)**, and the "stuck because / better by" framing. _Sources:_
[unit02 sources](research/unit02-theories-sources.md).
### Unit 3 — Ethics, Law & Professional Identity  ☑ readings + items
_Readings:_
- [unit03-ethics-law](wiki/unit03-ethics-law.md) — hub: the nine-section ACA Code map, five values +
  six principles, legal-vs-ethical duty, and the consent→confidentiality→exceptions chain.
- [concept-informed-consent](wiki/concept-informed-consent.md) — A.2: consent as ongoing process,
  required elements, minors/assent, mandated clients; form-first vs. conversation dialogue contrast.
- [concept-confidentiality-limits](wiki/concept-confidentiality-limits.md) — privacy vs.
  confidentiality vs. privilege (Jaffee), B.1–B.2 exceptions, minimal disclosure, **the
  confidentiality-speech worked model** (the syllabus practice rep).
- [concept-duty-to-warn](wiki/concept-duty-to-warn.md) — warn (1974) vs. protect (1976), the
  protective-steps menu, trigger criteria, state variation; threat-response dialogue contrast.
- [study-tarasoff](wiki/study-tarasoff.md) — the case: facts, both rulings, Tobriner's line, progeny
  (*Jablonski*, *Ewing*), *Jaffee* as the counterweight, the prediction critique.
- [concept-mandated-reporting](wiki/concept-mandated-reporting.md) — reasonable suspicion standard,
  immunity, penalties, vulnerable adults; **reporting vs. duty-to-warn table**; the honest-report
  dialogue.
- [concept-dual-relationships](wiki/concept-dual-relationships.md) — prohibited (A.5, the 5-year
  rule) vs. managed (A.6) relationships; crossing vs. violation; rural/small-community management.
- [concept-scope-of-practice](wiki/concept-scope-of-practice.md) — scope (legal) vs. competence
  (ethical, C.2); new specialties; values-based referral prohibition; impairment.
- [concept-helping-professions-compared](wiki/concept-helping-professions-compared.md) — counselor /
  psychologist / LCSW / psychiatrist table; who prescribes (RxP states); referral fluency.

_Items:_ [unit03-ethics-law](items/unit03-ethics-law.md) — 51 items across 5 clusters
(`confidentiality-exceptions` — the main interleaving target, `duty-concepts`, `ethics-principles`,
`boundary-concepts`, `the-helping-professions`), all 5 Bloom levels; heavy on which-exception
vignettes, compare items (9), and stance probes; includes the confidentiality-speech production rep.
_Sources:_ [unit03 sources](research/unit03-ethics-law-sources.md).

### Unit 4 — Multicultural & Social-Justice Competence  ☑ readings + items
_Readings:_
- [unit04-multicultural](wiki/unit04-multicultural.md) — hub: culture defined broadly (the counselor
  has one too), the three-framework map, the disparities/outcome evidence, the cultural-autobiography rep.
- [theory-tripartite-model](wiki/theory-tripartite-model.md) — Sue, Arredondo & McDavis (1992):
  awareness/knowledge/skills; one-client-three-failures dialogue contrast; the self-report validity problem.
- [concept-msjcc](wiki/concept-msjcc.md) — Ratts et al. (2015): four domains, privileged/marginalized
  quadrants, AKSA (action added), six socioecological advocacy levels; advocacy-scope debate.
- [concept-cultural-humility](wiki/concept-cultural-humility.md) — Tervalon & Murray-García's three
  commitments; competence-as-endpoint critique; Hook's client-rated outcome evidence; knowledge-as-hypothesis dialogue.
- [concept-acculturation](wiki/concept-acculturation.md) — Berry's 2×2 (integration/assimilation/
  separation/marginalization), acculturative stress, worldview, help-seeking norms; "resistance" reframe dialogue.
- [concept-privilege-power](wiki/concept-privilege-power.md) — McIntosh's knapsack, power in the dyad,
  Day-Vines' five broaching styles with worked broach dialogue.
- [concept-microaggressions](wiki/concept-microaggressions.md) — Sue's taxonomy, in-session
  rupture-and-repair dialogue, and the Lilienfeld critique presented both-sides.

_Items:_ [unit04-multicultural](items/unit04-multicultural.md) — 48 items across 5 clusters
(`mc-competence-dimensions`, `mc-frameworks`, `acculturation-strategies`, `broaching-styles`,
`microaggression-types`), all 5 Bloom levels; heavy on which-dimension/which-strategy/which-style
vignettes, compare items (6), stance probes, and two production reps (congruent broach; cultural
autobiography). _Sources:_ [unit04 sources](research/unit04-multicultural-sources.md).

### PFA & Acute Grief Support  ☑ readings + items  · borders Unit 8
_Readings:_
- [aux-psychological-first-aid](wiki/aux-psychological-first-aid.md) — hub: acute crisis support as a
  distinct skill, the "don't probe" rule, the stance, and the map. Anchor case: death of a child.
- [concept-pfa-core-actions](wiki/concept-pfa-core-actions.md) — WHO **Look/Listen/Link** + NCTSN's

- [concept-grief-models](wiki/concept-grief-models.md) — Kübler-Ross stages + their retirement;

- [concept-supporting-the-bereaved](wiki/concept-supporting-the-bereaved.md) — the child-death case:
  presence over fixing, say the name, **Ring Theory** ("comfort in, dump out"), when to refer, helper load.

_Items:_ [aux-psychological-first-aid](items/aux-psychological-first-aid.md) — 23 items across 3 clusters
(`pfa-frameworks`, `grief-models`, `bereavement-support`), all 5 Bloom levels; heavy on

[aux PFA sources](research/aux-psychological-first-aid-sources.md).
